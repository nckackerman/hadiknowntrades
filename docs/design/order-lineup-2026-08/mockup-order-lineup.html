Open questions for you

  1. Streak chips: both specs independently landed on the same answer — a small gold streak stat, shown only once a card is expanded/played (matching The Call Board's own shipped precedent), never a badge on the collapsed tile. Ready to lock in unless you want it off entirely.
  2. The Order's 4-attempt limit is a reasoned estimate (search-space math + ~30s/attempt pacing), not playtested. Feels right after playing it above, or too tight/loose?
  3. The Lineup's 7-round whole-board budget (bumped from an initial 6 once hidden length raised the difficulty) is likewise reasoned, not measured. After playing above, with the letter tracker in hand: still too tight, or does 7 leave room to spare?
  4. The Order's Mag 7-only pool trades daily variety for guaranteed recognizability — 5-of-7 gives 21 possible day-lineups, which may get repetitive within a couple of weeks. Worth widening the pool later (e.g. Mag 7 plus a rotating 8th "wildcard" slot), or is a small, totally-familiar pool actually the point?
  5. The Lineup's colmatch/rowmatch tile treatments (dashed neutral vs. dashed blue, above) are a first pass — telling them apart at a glance is now load-bearing for solving efficiently, not just decorative, so this deserves closer scrutiny than a typical color pass.
  6. New this round — the letter tracker (below the legend): showing only the single best-ever result per letter is a real simplification when a letter is exact in one column and absent in another. Does it help enough to keep, or does the simplification actually mislead?
  7. Win celebration: neither spec commits to full CelebrationBurst confetti reuse for either game — both propose a simple settled/earnest state for now (the length recap line is as far as this pass went). Worth revisiting once these are real?
